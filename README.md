## 🚀 Play Locally

1. Open your browser and navigate to:
   👉 **`http://localhost:8080`**
2. Or simply open `index.html` (for the Arcade Hub) or `games/02-wordle-survivor/index.html` in your browser.

## 🌐 Deploy to Cloudflare Pages (Free Worldwide CDN in 60s)

1. Sign up at [Cloudflare Dashboard](https://dash.cloudflare.com/).
2. Go to **Workers & Pages** $\to$ **Create application** $\to$ **Pages**.
3. Select **Direct Upload**, drop this `Quick Games` folder, and click **Deploy**.
4. You instantly receive an SSL-secured, zero-latency global URL (e.g. `https://quick-games.pages.dev`).
